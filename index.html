Why we exist

Good ideas rarely die from bad ideas. They die from missing pieces.

01

No one to sense-check the decision. Founders end up guessing at inflection points where an outside, experienced view would have settled it in an afternoon.

02

Nowhere to actually build and prove it. Testing an idea properly often needs infrastructure a small business can't yet justify buying.

03

Capital that assumes you already have capital. Formal investment networks are built for businesses that look a certain way on paper — not for what's actually promising.

—

Glean exists to fill in what's missing. We're not here to replace what you've built, or to run it for you. We're the soundboard, the sandbox, and the introduction — brought together in one place.

What we bring together

Three things that usually live in three different worlds.

01 — People

Expert guidance

Sit across from people who've run this exact playbook before. Strategy sessions, sector-specific advisors, and an honest sounding board for decisions that don't have an obvious answer yet.

02 — Infrastructure

Technology & sandboxing

Production-grade and sandbox environments to build, test, and prove your product — without provisioning infrastructure you can't yet justify owning.

03 — Capital

Funding pathways

Warm introductions to investors and funding structures suited to where your business actually is — not where a pitch deck template says it should be.
